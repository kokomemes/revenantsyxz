// REVENANTS / REWARDS

Hold one.
Access many.

A proposed holder rewards model funded by actual platform revenue—not newly minted $REVENANTS.

The proposed rewards flow.

Users pay for Revenants premium products and services.
After operating costs, an allocated portion of net revenue enters the reward treasury.
Treasury funds may be converted into supported Robinhood Chain assets.
Eligible $REVENANTS holders may claim allocations based on defined holding criteria.
// IMPORTANT

Rewards are a proposed feature, not currently live or guaranteed. Asset eligibility, availability, transfer restrictions, compliance, distribution rules, and allocation rates remain to be finalized.
